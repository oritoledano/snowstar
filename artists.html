Before you ask

The questions everyone asks.

Do I keep my rights?

All of them. Masters, publishing, and your writer's share of PRO royalties stay 100% yours — we never collect or touch them. You grant us a non-exclusive right to license your tracks to our clients, nothing more.

Is it exclusive?

No. Release the same tracks on Spotify, license them through other libraries, sell them yourself — all fine. If you leave, new sales stop; licences already sold stay valid and we keep paying you your share of them.

How do I get paid?

You get 50% of every licence fee your track earns. The sale shows in your dashboard the day it happens — named licence, real amount. Money goes out monthly; balances under ₪100 roll forward to the next month.

What about co-written tracks?

Declare your collaborators and their shares when you upload. Co-owned tracks sell by custom quote only, and every sale splits on the ledger exactly as declared.

Who sets the prices?

We do — every track is priced by the curator so the catalog stays coherent and nobody undercuts anybody. You can always ask us to revisit yours.
