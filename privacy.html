Privacy

Snowstar.Company — Tel Aviv

Last updated 14 August 2026

This page explains what Snowstar.Company collects when you use snowstar.company and the Mutra music catalog, why, and how to get rid of it. We have deliberately kept the collection small.

Visiting the site

We count page views and track plays so we know what people listen to. This is anonymous: a random id held only for the current browser tab, the country your request arrived from, and the site that referred you. We do not store IP addresses, we do not use advertising cookies, and we honour “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control — if either is on, we record nothing at all.

Accounts

If you create an account we store your email address, your name if you give one, and your saved favorites. Passwords are never stored — only a salted, stretched hash that cannot be reversed. If you sign in with Google or Facebook we receive your name, email address and profile picture from them, and nothing else; we never receive your password, and we cannot post anything or read your contacts.

Newsletter emails are only sent if you tick the box yourself. Ticking nothing means we never email you except about something you asked for, such as a password reset.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. The only third parties involved are the services that run the site — Cloudflare (hosting, storage and delivery), GitHub Pages (the pages themselves), and Resend (sending account emails such as password resets). They process data on our behalf, not for their own purposes.

Deleting your data

Email hello@snowstar.company and we will delete your account and everything attached to it — email address, name, favorites and sessions — within 30 days, usually the same day.

If you signed up through Facebook you can also use Settings → Apps and Websites → Snowstar → Remove. Facebook then notifies us automatically and the account is deleted on the spot.

Deletion is permanent. Anonymous, non-identifying counts (such as “this track was played 40 times”) remain, because they are not linked to any person.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@snowstar.company and a human will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept it.
