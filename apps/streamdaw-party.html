Questions.

Do guests need an app?

They scan the QR code and press Play in their browser. Nothing to install.

What about Bluetooth earphones?

Bluetooth adds its own delay. A guest on earphones can tap along to a tick to correct that phone.

Does a phone use its microphone?

Only to line up with the room, and only after its person says Line up. A phone that is not in the room listens without beeps or microphone.

How many phones can join?

Each phone gets its own stream from your Mac, so your upload sets the limit. In sync mode a phone takes about 0.15 Mbit/s, or about 1.5 on an iPhone with iOS 18 or older.

Who handles the music licence?

You do. Playing music at an event can need a licence where you hold it, usually from the rights societies there.
