How it works.

  1. Send the link and let your guest in from the waiting room.

Questions.

Does the take really land in time?

Yes, with earphones in. Before the first take the phone measures its own delay (3 soft beeps on its speaker, or 8 taps on earphones), and every take is placed by it. Record shows how the timing was found.

What if the connection stumbles?

The phone keeps a clean copy of every take, exactly as its microphone gave it, and sends it after the take. In Ableton Live 12 it replaces the live take on the track as one undo step.

Can someone play MIDI from far away?

Yes, into Ableton Live. A keyboard player plays their own keyboard in Chrome, or the keys and pads on their phone's screen. Each take lands in place as one clip.

Can we see and hear each other?

Yes: cameras, shared screens and talkback, up to 6 guests on camera.

Do I need a DAW?

Only to record into one. Record guests saves each person on an input to a file of their own in Music › StreamDAW › Guests.
