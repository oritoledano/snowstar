When Hush is the better choice.

  • You want to pay once. After about 18 months of Audioctor's yearly plan, Hush has cost you less.
  • You clean many audio files at a time. Audiobook chapters, voiceover auditions, dialogue stems. Hush takes a whole batch.
  • You work in Pro Tools. Hush Pro is an AudioSuite plugin made for post-production.
  • You're on macOS 12 or 13. Audioctor needs macOS 14.

Questions.

Is there a free alternative to Hush for Mac?

Audioctor is free to start and stays free for short clips. The free plan has every tool and no watermark. Your first 3 exports can be up to 6 minutes each, then 30-second clips, 3 a day.

How much does Audioctor cost compared with Hush?

Audioctor is $5 a month billed yearly or $7 monthly. Hush is a one-time purchase, $89.99 on the Mac App Store when we checked on 17 November 2026.

Can Audioctor clean the audio inside a video?

Yes. Drop the video, fix the sound, export the same video in sync. You don't need a video editor in between.

Does Audioctor run locally like Hush?

Yes. Everything runs on your Mac, and Audioctor works offline.

Can I control how much noise and reverb is removed?

Yes. Clean voice and Studio Voice are separate, each track has its own fader and EQ, and you can blend the original back in.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes, on macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs take longer than Apple silicon.
