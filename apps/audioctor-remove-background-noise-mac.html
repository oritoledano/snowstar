Three kinds of noise, one of them easy.

Which tool works depends on what the noise is. Listen to five seconds where nobody talks and you'll know which one you have.

  • Steady noiseA fan, air conditioning, fridge hum, mic hiss. Any noise reduction handles it, including the slider in iMovie.
  • Changing noiseTraffic, wind, a crowd, a dog, dishes. A classic noise filter can't follow it. This needs the voice separated from everything else.
  • Music under the voiceA café playlist or a radio. A filter treats it as part of the voice. Audioctor puts it on its own track so you can turn it off.

Clean voice is built for the second and third kind. If the problem is the room itself, see how to remove echo from a video.

Less noise next time.

From a studio that records voices for TV. None of it costs money.

  • Get the mic closer. Halve the distance to your mouth and your voice gets much louder while the noise stays the same.
  • Turn off what hums. Air conditioning, the fridge, a laptop fan next to the mic. You stop hearing them in the room, and the mic doesn't.
  • Put your back to the wind. Your body blocks more of it than a foam cover does.
  • Record ten seconds of silence first. Play it back on headphones. Whatever you hear will be under every word.

Questions.

How do I remove background noise from a video on a Mac?

Drop the video on Audioctor, press Clean voice, and export. You get the same video with the noise taken off the voice. It runs on your Mac and works offline.

Can it remove background music from a video?

Yes. Audioctor separates the voice and the music into their own tracks. Turn the music down or off and export.

Does it remove wind noise?

It takes most of it off. Very strong wind that overloads the mic also damages the voice itself, and no software brings that back fully.

Is it free?

Does my video get uploaded?
