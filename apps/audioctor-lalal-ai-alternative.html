When LALAL.AI is the better choice.

  • You're on Windows, Linux or a phone. Audioctor is Mac only.
  • You need synth, strings or wind on their own, or lead and backing vocals split apart. Audioctor has five stems.
  • You want to split inside your DAW. LALAL.AI has a VST plugin on Pro. Audioctor is a separate app.
  • You're building something on an API. LALAL.AI offers one.

Questions.

Is there an offline alternative to LALAL.AI for Mac?

Yes. Audioctor splits songs into vocal, drums, bass, guitars and keys on your Mac and works offline on every plan, including the free one.

Is Audioctor free?

Yes to start. The free plan has every tool and no watermark. Your first 3 exports can be up to 6 minutes each, then 30-second clips, 3 a day. Unlimited is $5 a month billed yearly or $7 monthly.

Which stems does Audioctor split?

Vocal, drums, bass, guitars and keys. Each one gets its own fader and exports as its own file.

Does my music get uploaded?

No. Everything runs on your Mac, and Audioctor works offline.

Are there minutes or credits?

No. The paid plan covers any number of songs of any length.

Does it work on Windows?

No. Audioctor is for Mac, macOS 14 or later. LALAL.AI has Windows and Linux apps.
