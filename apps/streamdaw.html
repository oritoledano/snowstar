Questions.

How good is the sound?

Lossless by default: uncompressed 16-bit at your session's sample rate, about 1.6 Mbit/s a listener. In sync mode, or when a listener's connection can't keep up, that listener gets high-quality Opus so nobody drops out.

How many people can listen?

Each listener gets their own stream from your Mac, so your upload sets the limit. A lossless listener takes about 1.6 Mbit/s, a phone in sync mode about 0.15: a 20 Mbit/s upload carries a review session of 10, or a band of 20 with room to spare. Up to 6 guests on camera.

Is everyone really in time?

Yes. In sync mode every phone plays the same instant, half a second behind your DAW, and a few short beeps line up the room's speakers too. Wired in-ears keep a band tightest; Bluetooth adds its own delay.

Which DAWs does it work in?

We have streamed sessions from Ableton Live and from the StreamDAW app. The plug-in loads as AU or VST3 in Logic Pro, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper and Bitwig, which we have not tested yet. Pro Tools is on the way.

Who can get into my session?

Only people you let in. Anyone with the link reaches your waiting room, and you let each person in. With Pro you can also require sign-in, so each listener registers first.

Is there a monthly plan?

No. Pro is one payment. If your trial is over and you need Pro for one more job, the 7-day pass costs $16, and what you paid comes off Pro if you buy within 30 days.

How does the licence work?

One payment, no subscription, and every StreamDAW 1 update included. Your key is made for your Mac and usually arrives within minutes: paste it into StreamDAW › Settings › Licence. New Mac? Sign in on the unlock page and request a key for it. Read the licence terms.
