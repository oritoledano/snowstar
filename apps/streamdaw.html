Questions.

How good is the sound?

Lossless by default: uncompressed 16-bit at your session's sample rate, about 1.6 Mbit/s a listener for the full mix at 44.1 or 48 kHz, twice that at 88.2 or 96. In sync mode, or when a listener's connection can't keep up, that listener gets Opus at 128 kbit/s. iPhones on iOS 18 or older can't play Opus, so they stay uncompressed.

How many people can listen?

Each listener gets their own stream from your Mac, so your upload sets the limit. At 44.1 or 48 kHz, a listener on the full mix takes about 1.6 Mbit/s, so a 20 Mbit/s upload carries about 10 with room to spare. Outside sync mode, a listener on the track mixer takes about 1.5 Mbit/s for every stem you send. With 8 stems, that is about 12 Mbit/s for one person. In sync mode a phone takes about 0.15 Mbit/s, or about 1.5 on an iPhone with iOS 18 or older. Up to 6 guests on camera.

Is everyone really in time?

Yes. In sync mode every phone plays the same instant, half a second behind your DAW, and a few short beeps line up the room's speakers too. Wired in-ears keep a band tightest; Bluetooth adds its own delay.

Which DAWs does it work with?

Tested in Ableton Live and the StreamDAW app. The plug-in loads as AU or VST3 in Logic Pro, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper and Bitwig. Pro Tools is on the way.

What does the installer put on my Mac?

The StreamDAW app, its plug-ins and the StreamDAW Audio device, and nothing else. Apple has checked the installer, so it opens with a double-click. The install guide shows every screen.

Who can get into my session?

Only people you let in. Anyone with the link reaches your waiting room, and you let each person in. With Pro you can also require sign-in, so each listener registers first. You can switch the waiting room off: the Silent disco preset starts with it off.

Your key is made for your Mac and names what you have, until when. It usually arrives within minutes: paste it into StreamDAW › Settings › Licence. Each month or year you pay, StreamDAW fetches the next key by itself while it is online. New Mac? Sign in on the unlock page and request a key for it. Bought Pro before? It stays yours, every product, for good. Read the licence terms.
