Questions

Straight answers.

Do listeners install anything?

No. The link or the QR code opens StreamDAW in their browser. To know exactly who is listening, require a Snowstar sign-in.

Which DAWs does it run in?

Any Mac host that loads AU or VST3: Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig. Pro Tools (AAX) is not supported yet.

How many people can listen?

As many as your internet upload carries: each listener gets their own stream from your Mac. Video is meant for a handful of people, and it steps down before anyone's audio suffers.

How good is the sound?

Your mix arrives lossless: the same samples your DAW plays, at your session's sample rate. On a weak connection, or in a personal mix, listeners get a high-quality compressed stream instead.

Is everyone really in time?

In in-ear mode every phone plays the same instant, half a second behind your DAW. A few short beeps, from the Mac or from a phone already in time, line the room's speakers and every phone up.

Can we use Bluetooth earphones?

They work, but each headset adds its own delay. Wired in-ears keep a band tight.

How does the licence work?

One payment, no subscription, every update included. The key is made for your Mac and arrives by email: paste it into StreamDAW › Settings › Licence. Moving to a new Mac? Sign in on this page and request a key for it.
