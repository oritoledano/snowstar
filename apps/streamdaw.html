Questions

Straight answers.

Do listeners need to install anything?

No. They open a link or scan the QR code. That's it.

How good is the sound?

Lossless, straight from your DAW. No MP3, no AAC, no bounce and re-upload: they hear the samples your session plays. In sync mode, or on a weak connection, they get a high-quality compressed stream instead, so nobody drops out.

How many people can listen?

Each listener gets their own stream from your Mac, so your upload sets the limit. A lossless listener takes about 1.6 Mbit/s, a phone in sync mode about 0.15: a 20 Mbit/s upload carries a review session of 10, or a band of 20 with room to spare. Up to 6 guests on camera.

Which DAWs does it work in?

Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig: any Mac DAW that loads AU or VST3. Pro Tools isn't supported yet.

Is everyone really in time?

Yes. In sync mode every phone plays the same instant, half a second behind your DAW, and a few short beeps line up the room's speakers too. Wired in-ears keep a band tightest: Bluetooth adds its own delay.

How does the licence work?

One payment, no subscription, and every StreamDAW 1 update included. Your key is made for your Mac and usually arrives within minutes: paste it into StreamDAW › Settings › Licence. New Mac? Sign in here and request a key for it. The licence terms.

Try before you buy: the free version and the 7-day Pro trial cost nothing. Once your key is issued, Pro isn't refundable, except for a double charge or a key that never arrives. Details.
