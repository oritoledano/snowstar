Your licence

Unlock your Mac.

Each key is made for one Mac. Getting yours takes a minute.

  1. In Audioctor, click the licence line at the bottom left of its window and press Copy next to This Mac.
  2. Paste it below and press Request my key.
  3. Copy the key, paste it into the same panel and press Unlock.
