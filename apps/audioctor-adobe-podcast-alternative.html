Questions.

Is Audioctor free?

Yes. The free plan has every tool and no watermark. Your first 3 exports can be up to 6 minutes each. After that you get 30-second clips, 3 a day. Unlimited is $5 a month billed yearly or $7 monthly.

Does my audio get uploaded?

No. Everything runs on your Mac, and Audioctor works offline.

Can it clean the audio inside a video?

Yes. Drop the video, fix the sound, export the video. You don't need an editor in between.

Will my voice sound robotic?

You control it. Blend the original back in and adjust the EQ until it sounds like you on a good day.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes, on macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs take longer than Apple silicon.

Does it work on Windows or iPhone?

Not yet. A phone app for creators is being built.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in your account or on the cancel page. You drop back to the free plan and keep all your files.
