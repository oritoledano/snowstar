Questions.

Do they hear my file as it is?

Yes. Each file arrives exactly as it is; an AIFF arrives as a WAV with the same samples.

Can we compare video?

Yes. Listeners watch two to four cuts side by side, or one big, every picture in time with your Play. The sound is the version they choose.

Where do the notes go?

They stay with the list from session to session. Past sessions keeps every list with its notes; Copy as text and Save CSV take them away.

Does it slow down a live stream?

No. Two listeners download at a time, and Compare never takes the upload your live stream needs.

Is there chat or video calling?

Compare leaves chat and cameras out, so the session stays on the versions and the notes.
