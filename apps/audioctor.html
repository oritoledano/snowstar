How it works

Drop. Mix. Shape.

01 · Drop

Drop any recording on the lab.

Audioctor hears what it is (speech, a song, one instrument) and takes it apart. Each part fills its own cylinder while the tubes show how far it is, to the second. One voice gives you one cylinder; a full song, up to seven.

02 · Mix

The liquid is the fader.

Drag a part up or down, switch it off, solo it, fold it to mono. The meters on the glass move with the music, and the waveform redraws as you mix. Before and After are matched in loudness, so you judge the change, not the volume.

03 · Shape

Process only where it helps.

Every part has a range EQ: choose from which frequency to which the processing acts, and how much. Keep a voice's own low end and clean only its top; let a little of the room back into the guitars. Then export the mix, or every part as its own file.

The chain

One chain, for any signal.

Four steps in order, each one a switch you can turn off, and every result kept: turning a step back on is instant.

Questions.

Does my audio leave my Mac?

No. Separation and every repair run on your Mac, on its graphics chip where they can. Nothing you open is uploaded, and Audioctor works offline.

How long does a song take?

On an M1 Max, Best takes about 15 seconds for each minute of audio, and Fast about 10. The first file after installing takes a little longer while macOS prepares the models. The tubes show the percentage and the time left as it goes.

What can it split?

Voice and music, or, for produced songs, voice, drums, bass, guitars, keys and the rest. Background always gets its own fader: noise, hum and whatever else isn't a part. A single voice or instrument stays one part, with nothing to split.

Will it change how my voice sounds?

Clean voice keeps your voice and moves the noise to Background. Studio voice rebuilds a voice and can change its colour, so it is a switch: compare Before and After, and keep what you like. The range EQ lets the processing act only where you choose.

Which DAWs?

The plug-in is an Audio Unit and a VST3, filed under snowstar.company: Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper and others. Drop files on its window, mix, and export, as in the app.

How does the licence work?

Like StreamDAW's: your key is made for your Mac and arrives by email within minutes; paste it into Audioctor. Monthly keys renew themselves while Audioctor is online. Each plan covers two Macs.

Can I get a refund?

Try it first: the 7 days cost nothing. Monthly: cancel any time; end it now instead and we refund the days you didn't use. Once your key is issued, a one-time purchase isn't refundable, except for a double charge or a key that never arrives.
