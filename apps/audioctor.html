Questions.

Does my audio leave my Mac?

No. Everything runs on your Mac, and Audioctor works offline.

Is there a plug-in?

Not yet. One that cleans the track it sits on is on its way. Until then, export from Audioctor and drop the files into your DAW.

My DJ software already splits stems. Why this?

Your DJ software splits them live, inside the set. Audioctor gives you each part as a file: a clean acapella, an instrumental, a drum loop, ready to edit, loop and EQ in your DAW.

Can I split any song?

On your own Mac, yes. Releasing someone else's song, or a part of it, needs the rights holder's permission, as it would with any other tool.

How fast is it?

On an M1 Max, about 15 seconds for each minute of speech, and about a minute to take a 3-minute song apart. Intel Macs take longer.

What's free?

Every tool, with no watermark. Your first 3 exports take clips up to 6 minutes each; after that, clips up to 30 seconds, 3 exports a day. Longer clips open, process and play for free; exporting them needs a plan.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in your account or on the cancel page. Nothing more is charged, and what you paid for runs to its end. Then you're back on the free plan with all your files.
