Install StreamDAW.

StreamDAW is downloading. Follow the steps below while it does. Didn't start? Download again

About five minutes, once. Here is every screen you'll see, and what to press.

What it installs.

The installer puts these on your Mac, and nothing else:

  • The StreamDAW app /Applications
  • The plug-in, Audio Units and VST3 /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins
  • StreamDAW Audio, the audio device your DAW sends stems to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL

It also removes older copies of StreamDAW. Nothing starts when you log in. Download it only from snowstar.company.

  1. Download.

    Get StreamDAW from the StreamDAW page (free, with a Snowstar account) or from the link in your purchase email. One file lands in your Downloads folder: StreamDAW-0.7.x.pkg.

  2. Open the installer.

    Quit your DAW first. Then double-click the .pkg file. Apple has checked it, so it opens right away.

  3. Install.

    The installer opens with a short welcome: click Continue. It shows the parts it will put on your Mac. Keep them all ticked, click Install and type your password. At the end Core Audio restarts, so sound stops for about a second. When it offers to move the installer to the Trash, either answer is fine.

  4. Open StreamDAW.

    Open your DAW, rescan plug-ins if it asks, put StreamDAW on your master bus and press Go live. The StreamDAW app opens by itself (it's in Applications too).

    This is the StreamDAW app's icon, in Applications and in your Dock while it runs.

  5. Say yes to what you use.

    StreamDAW asks for each permission the first time it needs it, and never before. Click Allow. Here is why each one is asked:

    Microphone
    When StreamDAW first opens. Your DAW's stems reach StreamDAW through StreamDAW Audio, which macOS counts as a microphone: without this, every stem is silent. It's also your own mic, when you talk to your listeners.
    Camera
    When you switch your camera on (Pro). Your listeners see you only while it's on.
    Screen
    When you share a window or a screen (Pro). macOS shows its own picker and you choose exactly which window. If it also asks about Screen & System Audio Recording, allow it.
    System audio
    When you play another app into your stream (Pro). Spotify or Music, for example. macOS calls it System Audio Recording Only. StreamDAW takes the sound of the app you pick, and only that app.

    Said no by mistake? System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone (or Camera, or Screen & System Audio Recording), then switch StreamDAW on. After an update macOS may ask again: click Allow again.

That's it.

Why Windows asks.

Windows trusts an installer it has seen on many PCs already, or one signed by a company that paid for a certificate. StreamDAW for Windows is new and made by a small studio, so SmartScreen has nothing to go on and asks you to confirm it yourself. We'll sign it once enough of you use it.

The installer puts these on your PC, and nothing else:

  • The StreamDAW app, and a Start menu entry called StreamDAW C:\Program Files\StreamDAW
  • The plug-in, VST3 C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\StreamDAW.vst3

Nothing starts when you log in. Download it only from snowstar.company.

  1. Download.

    Get StreamDAW from the StreamDAW page (free, with a Snowstar account) or from the link in your purchase email. One file lands in your Downloads folder: StreamDAW-0.7.1-Windows.exe, about 44 MB. It needs Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, or Windows 11, on a 64-bit PC.

  2. Open the installer.

    Quit your DAW first. Then double-click the .exe file. Windows may say it protected your PC and show one Don't run button, as if there were no other way.

    Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows asks once more whether to let the installer make changes to your PC: click Yes.

  3. Install.

    Click through the installer and let it finish. The app goes to C:\Program Files\StreamDAW and the plug-in to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, where every DAW looks for VST3 plug-ins.

  4. Open StreamDAW.

    Open your DAW, rescan plug-ins if it asks, put StreamDAW on your master bus and press Go live. The StreamDAW app opens by itself. This first Windows version streams your DAW, plays the sound of any app or of the whole PC into the stream, takes your microphone, and shows the Monitor.

    This is the StreamDAW app's icon, under StreamDAW in your Start menu and on the taskbar while it runs.

That's it.
