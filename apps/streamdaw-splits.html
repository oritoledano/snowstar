How it works.

  1. In your DAW, send your tracks to StreamDAW Audio's pairs: 3/4, 5/6 and up.
  2. Every pair your DAW plays into goes out by itself, named as in your DAW.
  3. Send the link. Each listener opens Track mixer and makes the mix their own.
  4. Set anyone's mix from your desk. Copy it and paste it to the next.

Questions.

How many stems can I send?

Up to 32. Every pair your DAW plays into goes out by itself; switch one off with its ON and it stays off.

How many people can listen?

Each listener gets their own stream from your Mac, so your upload sets the limit. With 8 stems a listener on Track mixer takes about 12 Mbit/s. In sync mode a phone takes about 0.15 Mbit/s, or about 1.5 on an iPhone with iOS 18 or older.

Can a band play in time on their phones?

Yes. In sync mode every phone plays the same instant, half a second behind your DAW. Wired in-ears keep a band tightest; Bluetooth adds its own delay.

What is the difference from Master?

Master sends one stereo mix. Splits sends each track and gives every listener a mix of their own.

Do listeners need an app?

They open the link in their browser. Nothing to install.
