StreamDAW Pro keys are made for one Mac. Getting yours takes a minute.

  1. In StreamDAW, open Settings › Licence and press Copy next to your machine ID.
  2. Paste it below and press Request my key.
  3. Copy the key, paste it into Settings › Licence and press Unlock.
