How it works.

  1. Pick an app for deck A: Spotify, Music, YouTube, Chrome or any other app.
  2. Add deck B beside it. The crossfader blends the two, and Cue lets you hear one side alone.
  3. Talk over the music. Guests you put on a Record input go out with it.
  4. Press the red button to record the show to Music › StreamDAW › Session.
  5. Press Go Live and send the link.

Questions.

Who handles the music licence?

You do. Playing music to an audience can need a licence where you broadcast, usually from the rights societies there. StreamDAW carries the sound and holds no licence for the music you play.

Do my guests hear the music?

Yes. Guests on air hear the music without the voices, so they never hear themselves a second late.

Where does the recording go?

The red button saves the whole show, the music and the conversation, to Music › StreamDAW › Session. A REC key on Stream also records exactly what your listeners hear as a 24-bit WAV.

Do I need a DAW?

No. Station plays the apps on your Mac. If you work in a DAW, you can still put it on a deck.

Can I hear one deck before it goes out?

Yes. Press Cue on one side of the crossfader to hear that program alone, while your listeners keep hearing the blend.
