How long a licence runs

The tier says what you may do; the term you choose at checkout says for how long. Terms are 6, 12, 24 or 36 months, and run from the date the licence is granted. The term recorded on your licence is the one that governs it.

Work published while the licence was live may stay published after it ends. You do not have to take down a video, a post or an episode because a term lapsed. What ends is your right to put the track into new material, and to run paid media behind it. Renew before the end date and there is no gap.

Exclusive rights — where nobody else may licence the track while you hold it — are arranged case by case. Ask us.

What is never included

  • Redistributing the audio file itself, or making it available for others to download.
  • Using a track as the main product — a ringtone, a sample pack, a beat store, an NFT, or anything where the music is what is being sold.
  • Training machine-learning or generative-audio models on our files.
  • Registering a track, or any part of it, with a content-identification system such as YouTube Content ID or Facebook Rights Manager. This is the one that causes real damage, so we state it plainly.
  • Use in material that is unlawful, or that promotes hatred or violence against people.
  • Claiming authorship of the music, or implying that the artist endorses you or your product.

Performance royalties

Your licence covers the synchronisation — the right to put the music with your pictures. Where a track is registered with a performing-rights society, public-performance and broadcast royalties remain the broadcaster's or venue's own obligation, settled on their account under the society's published tariff. They are never billed to you by us, and we do not collect them.

Content ID claims

Automated systems match audio, not paperwork, so a properly licensed track can still be claimed. Tell us the channels you publish on and we will whitelist them in advance. If a claim lands anyway, send it to licensing@snowstar.company and we will release it. We do not promise a claim will never appear — no one honestly can — we promise to clear it.

Tracks we cannot licence instantly

Some tracks are marked Get a quote rather than showing a price. That means someone other than us has a say in how the track is used commercially. We will come back to you with terms; we will not grant rights we do not hold.

Payment, VAT and invoicing

Prices are in shekels and exclude VAT. Israeli buyers are charged VAT at the rate in force on the date of payment, and receive a tax invoice (חשבונית מס/קבלה). Sales to customers outside Israel are zero-rated as an export; we may ask for evidence of your place of business, and where the evidence is unclear we charge Israeli VAT.

Card payments are handled by our payment provider. We never receive or store your card number.

Refunds
