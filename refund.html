Refunds

Snowstar.Company — Tel Aviv

Last updated 21 August 2026

A music licence is delivered the moment you pay: the rights are granted and the audio files are yours to download. There is nothing to send back, and we cannot un-know that you have heard the track. So we will say the awkward part first, plainly, rather than bury it.

We do not refund a licence because you changed your mind, because the project was cancelled, or because the track did not suit the edit in the end. Preview every track in full before you buy — the whole catalog is free to listen to, unlimited, without an account.

What we do refund, in full

  • A duplicate charge. If you were billed twice for the same licence, we refund the duplicate. You do not need to ask twice.
  • A payment that failed to deliver. If you were charged and the licence or the files never arrived, and we cannot deliver them promptly, you get your money back.
  • An unauthorised transaction. If someone used your card without permission, we refund it and cancel the licence.
  • A rights problem on our side. If it turns out we could not grant what we sold you, we refund it in full — regardless of how much time has passed.
  • The wrong tier, upward. If you bought a tier and then need a wider one, we credit what you already paid against the wider licence rather than charging you twice.

Quotes

Tracks marked Get a quote are not paid for up front. Nothing is charged until you have seen the terms and the price in writing and accepted them.

How to ask

Email licensing@snowstar.company with your order number. We reply within two business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; the card networks usually take a further 5–10 business days to show it.

Where a tax invoice was already issued, a refund is documented with a credit note (חשבונית זיכוי) against the original — the invoice itself is never deleted.

Your statutory rights

Nothing here reduces rights you have under Israeli consumer law. Note that the cancellation right for distance sales under the Consumer Protection Law does not extend to information products that can be recorded or reproduced once they have been supplied — which is what a downloadable licensed track is. If you think your circumstances are an exception, write to us and we will look at it properly rather than quote this paragraph back at you.
